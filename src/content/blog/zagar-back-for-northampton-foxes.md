**NORTHAMPTON will welcome back Slovenian star Matej Zagar for their next ROWE Motor Oil Premiership fixture at King’s Lynn on Monday.**

The former World No.5 has  been sidelined for two months having a suffered a broken ankle when he crashed out of just the Foxes’ fourth meeting of the season at Belle Vue on May 4.

Zagar comes back into the team when they race at the Adrian Flux Arena with Jonas Jeppesen, who was signed as his replacement, making way.

**Manager Chris Adams said:**

> “It’s good to have Matej back, he is fully fit now and will be riding in Poland on Sunday and he’ll then be back for us on Monday at King’s Lynn.
>
> “The door is still open for Jonas. He did a good job at Leicester on Thursday, and there are no hard feelings.
>
> “He knew what he was coming in for and what it entailed, and it’s just unfortunate for him that we have to let him go to make room for Matej to come back.”

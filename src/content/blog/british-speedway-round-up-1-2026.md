**PIC OF THE WEEK…**

**HERE’S the latest news and fixtures from around the tracks up and down the country.**

BELLE Vue star Dan Bewley delivered a sensational unbeaten Easter Monday performance as the Aces outscored Leicester in their Knockout Cup double.
Bewley collected a 15-point maximum in an impressive 57-33 home victory for his side at lunchtime, and was then unbeaten from six rides at the Hydroscand Arena in the evening, coming from the back on multiple occasions to rack up a 17+1 total.

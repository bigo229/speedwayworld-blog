# Dan Bewley Set for SGP Comeback in Vojens After Injury Return

> ### ⚡ Key Takeaways
> * **The Comeback:** Great Britain's **Dan Bewley** will make his official FIM Speedway Grand Prix return this Saturday at the Deluxe Homeart FIM Speedway GP of Denmark – Vojens.
> * **Strong League Return:** Bewley successfully returned to racing for Polish club **Wroclaw** on Sunday, scoring 10 paid 12 points following a three-month recovery from a broken femur.
> * **Play-off Updates:** Wroclaw dominant over Leszno (55-35), while Torun secured a vital 48-42 away win against Lublin in the PGE Ekstraliga semi-final first legs.

Great Britain star **Dan Bewley** makes his highly anticipated FIM Speedway Grand Prix comeback at the Deluxe Homeart FIM Speedway GP of Denmark – Vojens this Saturday.

The Cumbrian racer made his competitive return for Polish club Wroclaw in their PGE Ekstraliga play-off semi-final, first leg away to Leszno on Sunday. He scored an impressive 10 paid 12 points from five rides as the visitors won 55-35, putting one foot firmly in the final.

Bewley had been sidelined since May 20, when he suffered a broken femur and a compressed vertebra in his back during a crash at a Danish Speedwayligaen event in Outrup.

After months of intense rehabilitation, the world No.3 was elated to make his return. Posting images of the meeting on Facebook, he wrote: 

> "No words right now, just thank you to everyone who supported and believed in me the last three months."

## Wroclaw and Leszno Match Highlights

The high-stakes meeting also saw 2021 Speedway GP world champion **Artem Laguta** race to a perfect 15-point maximum for Wroclaw. 

Other notable Wroclaw performances included:
* **Maciej Janowski:** Starred with 11 points.
* **Mikkel Andersen:** The SGP2 series leader scored 7 points.
* **Brady Kurtz:** Bagged 6 paid 7 points.

Aussie racer **Ben Cook** topped Leszno’s scoring with 8 paid 9, while Piotr Pawlicki added 8 and Nazar Parnitskyi registered 2.

## Torun Edges Out Lublin in Semi-Final Thriller

In the other semi-final first leg, reigning champions **Torun** took a massive stride towards another final appearance after winning 48-42 away to their 2025 final rivals, Lublin.

Triple European champion **Mikkel Michelsen** led the Torun scoring charge on 12 points, while Emil Sayfutdinov accrued 11 paid 12, and Norick Blodorn collected 9 paid 11. Robert Lambert scored 6 and Patryk Dudek bagged 4 paid 5.

Speedway GP world champion **Bartosz Zmarzlik** topped the Lublin score chart with 14 points, while double SGP2 champion Mateusz Cierniak notched 9. SGP star Kacper Woryna added 8, and Fredrik Lindgren collected 4.

The definitive second leg for both PGE Ekstraliga ties will take place next Sunday.

**The current Individual World Champion Bartosz Zmarzlik will once again compete for the title of Sportsman of the Year in the annual “Przegląd Sportowy” poll.**

The Pole, who this past season secured his fifth gold medal in the Speedway Grand Prix series, has only once since 2016 not been among the nominees for the prestigious statuette. In 2019, he won and then consecutively finished in fifth, third, and twice second place, just behind Iga Świątek.

The results of the ongoing voting will be revealed during the Sports Champions Gala, which will take place on January 6, 2025.

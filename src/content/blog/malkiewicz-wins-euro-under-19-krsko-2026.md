**SGP2 star Kevin Malkiewicz raced to a sensational 15-point maximum to secure the 2026 European Under-19 Championship title at the historic Slovenian circuit in Krsko on Saturday.**

## Perfect Maximum Sees Malkiewicz Dominate in Slovenia

Grudziadz racer Malkiewicz went completely unbeaten at the former home of the FIM Slovenian Speedway GP. His flawless performance kept him clear of Lublin’s Bartosz Banbor, who secured second place overall on 14 points after being beaten only by Malkiewicz in his fourth heat. 

Germany’s Patrick Hyjek claimed the final step on the podium, taking third place with 12 points, while Denmark’s Jacob Jensen placed just behind in fourth on 11 points.

![Kevin Malkiewicz racing to victory at the 2026 European Under-19 Speedway Championship in Krsko](/images/blog/26SGP2LodzMeeting_Malkiewicz1-large-scaled.jpg)

## Heartbreak for Pawelczak as Brits Show Strong Form

Polish Under-21 champion Maksymilian Pawelczak was well on track for a podium finish after scoring 10 points from his opening four heats. Tragically, the SGP2 medal contender crashed out of his final ride, dashing his championship podium hopes and forcing him to finish fifth.

Meanwhile, the British duo of Luke Harrison and Cooper Rushen put in a highly commendable showing on the European stage. Harrison secured sixth place with nine points, followed closely by Rushen in seventh place with eight points. 

Further down the field, SGP2 star Will Cairns finished 13th on five points, Sweden’s Rasmus Karlsson took 14th with four points, and local Krsko favourite Sven Cerjak managed two points to finish 16th on his home track.

## 2026 European Under-19 Championship Results

* **1st:** Kevin Malkiewicz (Poland) – 15 points (Maximum)
* **2nd:** Bartosz Banbor (Poland) – 14 points
* **3rd:** Patrick Hyjek (Germany) – 12 points
* **4th:** Jacob Jensen (Denmark) – 11 points
* **5th:** Maksymilian Pawelczak (Poland) – 10 points

**FORMER Grand Prix star Peter Kildemand is returning to the ROWE Motor Oil Premiership with Oxford Spires next season.**

The 35-year-old Dane, who won two GPs in the mid-2010s, was part of Swindon’s victorious top-flight side in 2012.

His last spell in British Speedway was with Poole in 2018, although he had been due to race for King’s Lynn in 2022 before suffering a pre-season injury.

> Oxford manager Peter Schroeck said: “He is one of the most exciting guys in the Premiership.
>
> “Peter is a great addition to the Spires team, and everyone is really excited. I am sure that all of the Oxford fans will welcome his never-give-up attitude on track.”

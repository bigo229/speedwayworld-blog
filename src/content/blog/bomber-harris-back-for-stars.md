**KING’S Lynn have announced the return of Chris Harris for a second successive season with the club next year.**

Harris becomes the Stars’ first confirmed signing for the 2026 Premiership campaign after joining the club from Oxford last winter and helping them to the KO Cup Final and improved results in the top-flight.

**He said:**

> “It’s good to be back. As a rider I don’t like to keep jumping between different clubs, so it’s great to be settled and have a second season at King’s Lynn.
>
> “I still feel I’ve got unfinished business there after the season we had this year, and I enjoy the track and working with the people there, so it was an easy decision for me.
>
> “The aim of course is to win the league, and the club are certainly ambitious. The team they’re talking about is very exciting and I’m really looking forward to being a part of it, and hopefully we can fulfil the goals that the club deserve.”

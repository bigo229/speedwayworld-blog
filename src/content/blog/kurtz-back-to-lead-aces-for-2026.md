**WORLD No.2 Brady Kurtz will be back to lead Belle Vue in their chase for ROWE Motor Oil Premiership honours this season.**

The Australian enjoyed a stellar 2025 campaign, going agonisingly close to the sport’s biggest prize in his debut Grand Prix campaign.

With treble British Champion Dan Bewley also signed up for the Aces once more, it means the Manchester club will be tracking two of the world’s top three riders – with four of the top eight competing regularly in the UK.

**Kurtz said:**

> “I’m really looking forward to it again. It’s going to be my sixth season with the club and that’s pretty crazy to think I’ve been here that long!
>
> “I’ve enjoyed every year and hopefully this year will be no different.
>
> “I’ve had a really nice break over the winter in the few weeks I spent in Australia, and I’m really ready to go for the season again. I felt the break was needed and hopefully that will be the right choice.”

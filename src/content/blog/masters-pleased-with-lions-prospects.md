**LEICESTER star Sam Masters says the pieces should be in place for another strong season at the Hydroscand Arena, Beaumont Park this season.**

Masters was part of the Lions side which reached the ROWE Motor Oil Premiership Grand Final in 2024, and there is just one change to their line-up this year with Kyle Howarth replacing Richard Lawson.

Said Masters: “We had a lot of boys who were new to the club at Leicester, and it took us a bit to find our feet, but once we did we were a pretty dangerous team, so we’ll see how we go this year.

“Kyle is a lovely fellow, he gives 110 per cent, 120 per cent even, and he’s always a good laugh too, so we’ve still got that team spirit that I think helped us get to the Final last year.

“We’ll miss Lawson, he was a really good guy to have in the team and I enjoyed racing with him so he’ll be missed for sure, but we’ve still got a good team again and we’ve got to look forward.

“We’ve got to aim to get to the play-offs first, and do similar to last year, but one better would be nice!”

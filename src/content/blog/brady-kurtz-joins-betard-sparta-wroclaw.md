**The silver medalists of the PGE Ekstraliga 2024 have introduced a new player for the next season. The BETARD SPARTA team will be strengthened by Brady Kurtz, who was a leading figure in the METALKAS 2. Ekstraliga this year.**

– It’s my first day, but I already feel at home – this is how the Australian introduced himself in a short video from the Wrocław club. Thus, the best player of the last season of METALKAS 2. Ekstraliga returns to the top league. The last time in the PGE Ekstraliga Kurtz played for FOGO UNIA Leszno during the years 2018-2020.

The past season in the league’s second tier Brady Kurtz finished with a run-point average of 2.402, which allowed him to secure the 1st place in the individual rankings. His team, INNPRO ROW Rybnik, thanks in part to his performance, recently advanced to the PGE Ekstraliga.

– I think it was the best choice we could have made. A top-class player with considerable experience and great potential is joining BETARD SPARTA. Brady will be our reinforcement, a strong point in the senior lineup. I am convinced that he will quickly acclimate in Wrocław because our team includes players he knows well. Their recommendations were important to us as we prepared to assemble the lineup for the next season – said WTS president Andrzej Rusko in an interview for wts.pl. – In this way, in 2025, our colors will be represented by the best player of the PGE Ekstraliga and the best player of the METALKAS 2. Ekstraliga – he added.

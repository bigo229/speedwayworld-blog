**GERMAN international star Norick Blodorn will be back with Belle Vue in the Premiership this season.**

The 21-year-old has already spent four years with the Aces and he was a title-winner with the Manchester club in 2022 and 2024.

Last season Blodorn ended the domestic campaign with an average of 4.87, before securing a huge result when he led his country to a surprise victory in the FIM Speedway of Nations 2 at Torun.

He joins Dan Bewley and new Rising Star Will Cairns as confirmed signings at the National Speedway Stadium for 2026.

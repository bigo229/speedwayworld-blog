BEN Fund Bonanza ever-present Ben Barker will keep up his record in Saturday’s 16th staging of the event at Glasgow.

Three more current Tigers riders – Leon Flint, Sam Hagon and Kevin Juhl Pedersen – have also joined the line-up for the fund-raising event.

Pedersen steps in for Adam Ellis who is now unavailable, whilst Edinburgh’s Kye Thomson has accepted an invitation to replace the injured Charles Wright.

**Barker said:**

> “The Ben Fund’s close to my heart and that’s why I’ve done it every year. I don’t know how many more we’ve got left though!
>
> “I’ve benefitted from the fund throughout my riding years and that’s why you’ve always got to support it. I finished the season injured last year.
>
> “I’m happy it’s at Glasgow, I love the stadium and I think they’ve done a great job there so it’s good to go up there and do a Bonanza.”

Full line-up: Chris Harris, Peter Kildemand, Tom Brennan, Kyle Howarth, Justin Sedgmen, Nick Morris, Tate Zischke, Danny King, Dan Thompson, Kevin Juhl Pedersen, Victor Palovaara, Dan Gilkes, Ben Barker, Sam Hagon, Leon Flint, Kye Thomson. Reserve: Ace Pijper.

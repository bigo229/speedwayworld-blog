**RYAN Douglas has sealed a return to Premiership runners-up Leicester as the club’s third signing for 2026.**

The Australian, who has represented the Lions at both senior levels, enjoyed his best-ever season this year, raising his starting average by nearly a point to 8.12 making him one of the top performers in the league.

**Douglas said:**

> “I’ve always enjoyed being part of Leicester, both at Championship and Premiership level, and when the opportunity came to return it really was an easy decision for me.
>
> “Working with Stewart (Dickson) is great and there’s always a good team atmosphere, which makes a difference throughout the season.
>
> “It looks like I’ll be riding at No.1, which is an exciting challenge. I feel like I had a breakthrough with my home performances last season and I believe there’s still more improvement to come.”

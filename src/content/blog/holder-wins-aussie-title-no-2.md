**Speedway GP star Jack Holder stormed to his second Australian Championship as he completed a clean sweep of wins at Adelaide track Gillman on Friday.**

The world No.5 won all four rounds of the championship on 72 points overall. He also topped the podium at the double-header in Albury-Wodonga last weekend and won in Mildura on Wednesday.

Victory sees Holder add to the Aussie crown he claimed in 2023. He also earns one of Australia’s places in the Speedway GP qualification rounds, where he is joined by runner-up Jaimon Lidsey and the winner of the FIM Oceania Championship, which takes place at Gillman on Sunday.

Holder lifted the Aussie Championship before the Gillman final had even been staged. But this didn’t break the Sheffield and Gorzow rider’s concentration as Holder finished the job by winning the final ahead of Tungate, who blazed through the heats with a 15-point maximum. Tungate claimed third place in the overall standings on 61.

Lidsey was third on the night as he secured second spot in the overall standings on 64 points – just fending off Tungate for the SGP qualifying round spot.

Despite Zach Cook finishing the Gillman round with nine championship points, he took fourth spot in the overall standings on 41, with former SGP2 star Keynan Rew taking fifth place on 39.

Ben Cook finished fourth in the Gillman round as he bounced back after missing the Mildura meeting due to illness, taking sixth overall on 37 ahead of Jacob Hook, who ended an impressive series on 31 in seventh place.

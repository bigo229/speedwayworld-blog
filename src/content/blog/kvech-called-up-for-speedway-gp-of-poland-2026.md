**Czech charger Jan Kvech steps into the FIM Speedway GP of Poland – Lodz line-up next Saturday after Britain’s Dan Bewley officially withdrew.**

The Cumbrian continues his recovery from the broken femur he suffered in a Danish Speedwayligaen in Outrup on May 20.

His absence hands second substitute Kvech the opportunity to compete in a sixth straight round as he joins first reserve Anders Thomsen on track, with the Dane replacing the absent Fredrik Lindgren.

We wish Dan and Fredrik a speedy recovery.
